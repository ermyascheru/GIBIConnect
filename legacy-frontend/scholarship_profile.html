info Grant Mission & Funding Overview

The Ethiopian Excellence Graduate Grant is a prestigious national initiative launched by the Ministry of Education (MoE) in collaboration with Addis Ababa University. Designed specifically for outstanding graduate researchers, this grant aims to elevate the standard of higher education and industrial research in Ethiopia.

With an emphasis on Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM), the program seeks to empower high-achieving scholars with full tuition waivers, research lab budgets, and a monthly living allowance of 15,000 ETB, allowing dedicated focus on scientific breakthroughs.

Recipients commit to contributing to national priority sectors upon graduation, applying advanced methodologies to solve regional challenges in sustainable energy, healthcare informatics, artificial intelligence, and agricultural technology.
