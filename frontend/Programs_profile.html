info Program Overview & Curriculum Mission

The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at Addis Ababa University is designed to train world-class researchers, engineers, and data scientists capable of developing transformative AI solutions tailored for Ethiopia and the broader African continent.

The curriculum blends theoretical rigor in deep learning, statistical learning theory, and reinforcement learning with applied research in natural language processing (Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya), autonomous systems, remote sensing, and healthcare diagnostics.
