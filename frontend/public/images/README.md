# GIBIConnect - Authentic Campus & University Image Assets Guide

To replace images with your own local real Ethiopian university and student photography, simply place image files in the folders below. The frontend will automatically load them!

## Folder Structure & Recommended File Names

### 1. Universities (`/public/images/universities/`)
- `aau-hero.jpg` — Addis Ababa University (Sidist Kilo / Ras Makonnen Hall)
- `aau-tour.jpg` — AAU Campus Walkway / Fountain
- `aau-landmark.jpg` — AAU JFK Memorial Library or Museum
- `astu-hero.jpg` — Adama Science and Technology University Main Gate / Complex
- `astu-tour.jpg` — ASTU Center of Excellence Labs
- `ju-hero.jpg` — Jimma University Main Campus
- `ju-medical.jpg` — Jimma University Specialized Hospital (JUMC)
- `bdu-hero.jpg` — Bahir Dar University Lake Tana Shore Campus
- `aastu-hero.jpg` — Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (Kilinto)
- `uog-hero.jpg` — University of Gondar Maraki Campus
- `hu-hero.jpg` — Hawassa University Main Campus
- `haramaya-hero.jpg` — Haramaya University Agriculture Complex

### 2. Facilities (`/public/images/facilities/`)
- `library-reading-hall.jpg` — Central University Library Stacks & Reading Desks
- `engineering-lab.jpg` — Robotics, Electronics & Mechatronics Testing Lab
- `hospital-clinical.jpg` — Teaching Referral Hospital & Clinical Training Ward
- `student-dorm.jpg` — Ethiopian University Dormitory Blocks & Dining Hall
- `sports-stadium.jpg` — Olympic Athletics Running Track & Sports Arena
- `ict-datacenter.jpg` — High Performance ICT Server Data Center

### 3. Students & Community (`/public/images/students/`)
- `ethiopian-students-group.jpg` — Ethiopian university students studying together
- `student-graduates.jpg` — Ethiopian graduation ceremony
- `tutor-1.jpg`, `tutor-2.jpg`, `tutor-3.jpg` — Peer tutors and academic advisors

### 4. Events (`/public/images/events/`)
- `hackathon.jpg` — National University Tech Hackathon
- `symposium.jpg` — Higher Education Medical & Engineering Research Symposium
- `career-fair.jpg` — Annual University Career & Internship Expo

---
*Recommended resolution for Hero banners: 1920x1080 (JPG or WebP)*  
*Recommended resolution for Facility/Card photos: 1200x800 (JPG or WebP)*
